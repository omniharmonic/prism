What it does

An agent that shows its work.

Omni is the agent we run on our own Prism Server today. We're preparing a public version anyone can run on theirs.

  • ✓

    Cards, not chores

    Each card holds finished work you can read in full: approve, revise or discard it with one tap.

  • ☀

    Briefings before you need them

    Before a meeting, Omni pulls together who's coming, what you last agreed and what's still open.

  • ✉

    Drafts in your voice

    Omni can draft replies to messages that are waiting on you. Nothing is sent until you approve it.

  • ◷

    Works while you don't

    Ask for something and close the app. Omni tells you when it's done, or when it needs you.

  • ◉

    Meetings, with your permission

    Omni always asks before it joins a call, then hands the recording to Minutes for notes.

  • ⛨

    On a leash

    Omni sees only what your server lets it see, and every outgoing message goes through the same approval door.

Private by design

  • Your data stays on your server. The Omni app talks only to your Prism Server; the agent runs on hardware you control.
  • You choose the AI model. The text a request needs goes only to the model provider you configure (or stays on the Mac with a local model).
  • No tracking, no third-party sharing. No analytics or ads in the app, and nothing is sent anywhere without your approval.

The details are in the Omni privacy notes.
