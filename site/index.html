Why Prism

You shouldn't have to choose
between polish and ownership.

Notion made workspaces beautiful and collaborative. Obsidian made them yours. Prism is what happens when you refuse to pick one.

The power

Everything you'd miss from Notion

  • Blocks for everything. Headings, to-dos, toggles, columns, tables, callouts, embeds — from one / menu.
  • Real databases. Typed properties, filters, sorts, totals; table, board, calendar, gallery and list views.
  • Live together. Cursors, comments, and suggested edits someone else accepts.
  • Share precisely. A page, a folder or a tag — as viewer, commenter, suggester or editor. Or publish a tag as a public wiki.

The soul

Everything you love about Obsidian

  • Your vault, your machine. Notes live in a Parachute vault — a knowledge graph on your own Mac.
  • [[Wikilinks]] that autocomplete. Backlinks on every page, and a graph that grows with you.
  • Keyboard-first. ⌘K to go anywhere, [[ to link, shortcuts for the rest.
  • Markdown in and out. Offline. Import Markdown or a Notion export; export a page or the whole workspace. Edit offline; it syncs when you're back.

For people who love Obsidian

Your notes, as a living graph.

Every [[link]] and mention becomes an edge. Meetings connect to people, people to projects, projects to the pages that matter. Open any page and see what points back at it.

Hover a note to light up its neighbours. Drag one to pull the web.

Your data, your vault

The key never leaves your server.

Prism has no cloud of its own. Your apps talk to Prism Server on your Mac; only the server holds the key to your Parachute vault, and it checks every request against your permissions first.

  • Self-hosted. Your server and your vault run on hardware you own.
  • Apps never hold the vault key. Revoke any device from Settings at any time.
  • Permissions on every request. Shared pages, private pages and links are checked by the server, not trusted from a client.

Get started

Your own server in an afternoon.

Setup is done by an agent: run Claude Code, ask it to “set up Prism”, and it walks you through each step while Prism's own checklist ticks them off. Stop and resume any time.

  1. 01

    Run the host bundle

    On an Apple-silicon Mac with macOS 14 or newer, unpack the host bundle and start the setup run.

    ./prism-host-*/bin/prism setup
  2. 02

    Connect your vault

    Setup finds your Parachute vault — or installs it with your yes — and hands the server its key directly. You never see it, so it never lands in a chat.

  3. 03

    Sign in everywhere

    Sign in as the owner, install Prism for Mac, sign in your phone. Add mail, calendar, chat and transcripts when you're ready.
