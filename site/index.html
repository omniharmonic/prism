Your data, your vault

Your hardware. Your rules.

The people who make Prism never see your notes or messages. There is no account to make with us, and no analytics in the apps or on this site.

  • Self-hosted. Prism Server and your Parachute vault run on a Mac you own. Back it up, move it, or switch it off.
  • Apps never hold the vault key. Each device gets its own sign-in that you can revoke from Settings at any time.
  • One door out. Every message an app sends leaves through your server, behind a switch that stays off until you turn it on.
  • Open source. AGPL-3.0. Read, change and run every line yourself.

Get started

Your own server in an afternoon.

Setup is done by an agent: run Claude Code, ask it to “set up Prism”, and it walks you through each step while Prism's own checklist ticks them off. Stop and resume any time.

  1. 01

    Run the host bundle

    On an Apple-silicon Mac with macOS 14 or newer, unpack the host bundle and start the setup run.

    ./prism-host-*/bin/prism setup
  2. 02

    Connect your vault

    Setup finds your Parachute vault, or installs it with your yes, and hands the server its key directly. You never see it, so it never lands in a chat.

  3. 03

    Add the apps you want

    Sign in to Prism on your Mac and phone, then turn on Comms, pair Minutes and connect mail, calendar and chat when you're ready. The checklist shows which apps are set up.
