What it does

Made for the way you actually talk.

Comms is a thin app over your Prism Server's messaging module. The server brings in each network you connect, and every conversation lands in your vault next to the people and projects it belongs to.

  • ✉

    Every network, one list

    Email, iMessage and SMS (through a Mac signed in to Messages), plus WhatsApp, Signal, Telegram, Slack, Instagram and LinkedIn through bridges you run.

  • ◎

    Sorted by what needs you

    Filter to what's urgent or waiting on a reply. Folders and pins keep the rest tidy, and strangers and spam never buzz.

  • ♥

    Feels like Messages

    Bubbles, reactions, photos, files and voice messages, on Mac and iPhone, in a design that follows Apple's own Messages.

  • ◔

    Notifications that matter

    Only the threads you care about buzz. Mute a thread, choose per folder, or hide previews so a notification says only “New message”.

  • ◐

    Linked to people

    Each conversation links to the person's page in your vault, so one person's email, chats and meetings sit together.

  • ⛨

    Sending is yours to switch on

    Comms can only send once you turn sending on for your server. Every message leaves through that one door.

Private by design

  • Your messages stay on your server. Comms talks only to the Prism Server you sign in to. We never receive your conversations.
  • No tracking. No analytics, ads or third-party trackers in the app.
  • No sharing with third parties. Messages travel only over the networks you connected, the way they always do.

The details are in the Comms privacy notes.
