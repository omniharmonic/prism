Meeting notes, on device

Every meeting. Stays on your device.

Minutes records a call or a room, transcribes it with Apple's on-device speech recognition, tells the speakers apart and writes notes. There's no account and no Minutes server. Connect Prism and the notes land in your vault.

What it does

Notes you can trust, from audio that stays put.

Minutes is a native app for Mac and iPhone. It works on its own, and works better with Prism.

  • ●

    Record anything

    A video call on your Mac (its audio, never the screen), a room through the microphone, or a voice memo on your iPhone.

  • ≋

    Transcribed on the device

    Apple's on-device speech recognition only. If a device can't do that, Minutes tells you instead of using a cloud service.

  • ⁂

    Who said what

    On-device speaker separation. You review the names, and remembering a voice is a separate choice you can undo.

  • ▦

    Knows your calendar

    Connect Apple Calendar or Google Calendar (read only) to name meetings and see who was invited.

  • ⌕

    Search every word

    Find a phrase in one meeting or across your whole library, right on the device.

  • →

    Into your vault

    Pair Minutes with your Prism Server and each meeting's notes and transcript arrive in your vault, linked to the people in it.

Private by design

  • Audio stays on the device. Recording, transcription and speaker separation all happen on your Mac or iPhone.
  • No account, no tracking. No Minutes server, no analytics, no ads.
  • You decide what leaves. Only iCloud sync to your own iCloud, a calendar you connect, or an export to a place you choose, like your Prism Server.

The details are in the Minutes privacy notes.

Stop taking notes. Start listening.
